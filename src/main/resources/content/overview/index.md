What h5m is, why it exists, and how it compares to Horreum.
|}

h5m is a lightweight reimagining of [Horreum](https://horreum.hyperfoil.io) that replaces its complex multi-entity data model with a simple directed acyclic graph (DAG) of computation nodes.
